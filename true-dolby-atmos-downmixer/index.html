+ Detail recovery pass: quiet/mid-panned background content re-extracted and blended back at full level before any limiting.
Why the old version lost background instruments: The side-channel encoding approach used (L−R)/2 to carry spatial data — but instruments panned near centre have nearly equal L/R energy, so they cancel out in the side signal and disappear. This version uses a full additive fold — every channel is summed with equal-power coefficients so nothing is ever subtracted or cancelled. A separate detail recovery pass re-extracts quiet mid-panned content and blends it back at 100% level before any processing touches it.
