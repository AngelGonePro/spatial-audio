Why the old version lost instruments — and what's fixed:
The old upmixer used (L−R)/2 as the source for ALL surround and rear channels. Any instrument panned centre or near-centre has almost equal L and R energy — so L−R ≈ 0 and those instruments completely disappeared from every surround channel.

New approach — fully additive, zero cancellation:
• SL/SR and BL/BR are now built from the full stereo Mid signal (L+R)/2 — this contains every instrument including centre-panned backgrounds.
• Width and spatial feel is added on top via delay, reverb, and EQ — never by subtraction.
• A separate detail recovery layer blends extra quiet-content back into all channels additively.
• FL/FR are a bit-perfect copy — zero processing, zero coloration.
