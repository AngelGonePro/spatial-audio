Mix Formula
SL out = SL × 1.0 + BL × fold_coeff (equal-power, BL delayed 0.5ms to prevent comb)
SR out = SR × 1.0 + BR × fold_coeff (equal-power, BR delayed 0.5ms to prevent comb)
FL / FR / FC / LFE = direct passthrough — zero processing
Equal-power fold — why it matters: A naive SL_out = SL + BL at equal amplitude causes a +3dB level jump and potential comb filtering when SL and BL have similar content. This uses equal-power coefficients (×0.707 each) so the combined energy stays at 0dB. A tiny 0.5ms pre-delay on BL/BR before folding decorrelates the two signals just enough to eliminate comb notches — too small to hear as echo, large enough to break phase alignment. All small details in both channels survive because nothing is ever subtracted.
