Derivation Formula
BL = SL × bl_level → delay(rear_ms) → LPF(10kHz) → reverb → dark EQ
BR = SR × bl_level → delay(rear_ms+2ms asymm.) → LPF(10kHz) → reverb → dark EQ
FL / FR / FC / LFE / SL / SR = bit-perfect copy — zero processing
How BL/BR are made to feel like real rear channels:
1. Delay — 8–20ms delay makes the brain perceive the signal as coming from behind (based on the Haas precedence effect). The two channels have a 2ms asymmetry for naturalness.
2. Low-pass filter — real rear speakers in cinema are rolled off above 7–10kHz. This mimics that characteristic, making BL/BR sound distinctly "rearward" vs SL/SR.
3. Reverb diffusion — longer, more diffuse tails vs the surrounds create an enveloping rear soundfield.
4. Detail recovery — a separate SL/SR copy is highpassed and blended back at low level to ensure no quiet background content is lost in the filtering.
All operations are additive — nothing is ever subtracted from the source signal.
